About

Torch Music Group was founded on a simple conviction: the people who built this industry held themselves to a standard that most of the business has abandoned. They studied the craft. They showed up every day. They treated greatness as a discipline, not a destination.

TMG exists to carry that standard forward. We are an artist development company, a publishing house, and a creative infrastructure built for people who are committed to the work.

We operate across management, publishing, and premium studio services through a vertically integrated model — because artists deserve a team that controls quality at every level.

If you’re here, it’s because you believe what we believe: that only trustworthy bearers carry the torch.

Artist Development & Management

Career architecture from the ground up. Creative direction, release strategy, brand building, and business education for artists who are ready to be developed — not just distributed.

Publishing

Catalog administration, co-publishing, and songwriter development.

Creative Infrastructure

Premium recording, A&R, and operational systems designed to give every project the environment and support it deserves. Anchored by Torch ATL.
