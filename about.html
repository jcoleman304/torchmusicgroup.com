About

Torch Music Group is an Atlanta-based music company built around three connected pillars: management, A&R and artist development, and publishing.

We believe artists are creative companies, careers are built record by record, and development is the work, not a phase. Every decision either compounds the career or costs it.

TMG is where talent is developed, careers are built, and legacies are made.

The Founder

Torch Music Group was founded by Joi Coleman — a Grammy-nominated songwriter with nine years at Atlantic Records across A&R and studio operations.

Joi built her career inside the machine. TMG is what she’s building next.

The Vision

A company built for the long arc of an artist’s career, and the longer arc of culture.

We don’t fear the fire. We are it.
— Torch Music Group
