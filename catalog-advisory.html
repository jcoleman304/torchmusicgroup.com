Catalog Advisory

Your catalog is an asset. We treat it like one.

Torch Music Group advises songwriters, producers, artists and estates on the value and sale of their music rights. We work across publishing, writer’s share, master royalties, and neighboring rights income.

What We Do

Catalog valuation analysis

We review royalty statements across all payors to establish what a catalog actually earns and what the market will pay for it.

Sell-side representation

We prepare the data room, position the catalog, and bring it to qualified buyers.

Buyer sourcing

We connect institutional and private buyers with vetted, diligence-ready catalogs.

Deal structuring

We take a deal from LOI through close, including full sales, partial sales, and royalty advances.
