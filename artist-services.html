Who You’re Working With

Joi Coleman spent nine years inside the Atlantic Records system, five of them as an A&R, signing and developing artists and making records at the major-label level.

Her A&R work includes Big Scarr, Enchanting, Pooh Shiesty, Raiche, Quando Rondo, Bunna B, Rockout Danny, RNB.FOEMOB and more. She earned a Grammy nomination as a songwriter on Dreamville’s Revenge of the Dreamers III.

She founded Torch Music Group to bring that experience to artists building independently. You get the same honest read a label A&R gives a signed artist: what is working, what is not, and what to do next.

Who It’s For

Artists

Independent artists releasing music who want an honest read on their records, their brand, and the plan behind the next release.

Songwriters

Writers who want to understand placements, publishing, splits, and how to position their catalog and pen for the rooms they want to be in.

Producers

Producers building a sound and a client list who need a strategy for placements, relationships, and getting paid properly on the records they make.

What the Artist Audit Covers

Your music

A professional listen and honest feedback on your records, from an A&R perspective, with notes on what is working and what is holding you back.

Your career goals

Where you want to be in twelve months, whether that is a label deal, a publishing deal, a touring base, or a profitable independent run, and what it actually takes to get there.

Your current strategy

Release cadence, content, team, audience, and distribution. We identify the gaps between what you are doing now and what the goal requires, including how to approach shows, radio interviews and press.

Your next steps

A clear, prioritized set of actions to take after the call, so you spend the next ninety days on the things that move you forward.
